Combat and buffs

Party attack combines base stats, level, IV, rarity, passive, awakening, job, equipment, perks, breeding, collection, and elemental effects.

  • Ribbon Bat Rally: +14% party attack for 5s every 7s.
  • Its +7% personal attack passive is separate from the active buff.
  • 50% CDR is the cap; permanent Rally uptime starts at 28.57% CDR.

Skill descriptions

Attack xN is the active skill power. For nukes, it multiplies the caster's resolved attack, then gear/perk/job skill power adds another multiplier.

  • Nuke: monsterAtk × active power × skill-power multiplier, then buffs, element, role, boss/trial, and crit effects.
  • Awakening changes skill power and cooldown separately from skill-power gear.
  • Multi-hit uses power × hits; DoT spreads the result over time.
  • Buff power is direct: Ribbon Bat Rally's 0.14 means +14%, and ordinary skill power does not scale it.
  • Passive attack and active skill power are separate effects.

Scaling stats

After capped bonuses are filled, prioritize stats that continue improving damage, uptime, or progression.

  • Attack: raises normal-hit and skill damage.
  • Skill power: raises eligible direct-damage skills and some skill effects.
  • Flat and percentage attack: both remain useful because they feed the attack formula.
  • Element advantage and elemental attack: valuable against matching targets.
  • Attack frequency: attack speed, extra hits, and damage-over-time effects improve DPS.
  • Awakening, evolution, level, IVs, breeding, and collection bonuses improve combat output.
